Global 5 Core Earth Year · 8 Mountain Month

The global Core Earth year centres on collective reassessment and turning points. Institutions, leadership and public trust may be tested, making calm reflection and a willingness to examine underlying imbalances especially valuable.

A Mountain month slows the collective pace towards consolidation and reflection. Defensive positions or delays may become more apparent; patient reassessment can reveal what needs a firmer foundation. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through boundaries and consolidation.
