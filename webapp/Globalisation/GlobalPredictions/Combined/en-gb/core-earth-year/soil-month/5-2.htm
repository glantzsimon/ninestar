Global 5 Core Earth Year · 2 Soil Month

The global Core Earth year centres on collective reassessment and turning points. Institutions, leadership and public trust may be tested, making calm reflection and a willingness to examine underlying imbalances especially valuable.

A Soil month brings practical support, maintenance and domestic or humanitarian concerns into focus. Modest cooperation and attention to existing commitments can offer steadier progress than dramatic announcements. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through practical care and stabilisation.
