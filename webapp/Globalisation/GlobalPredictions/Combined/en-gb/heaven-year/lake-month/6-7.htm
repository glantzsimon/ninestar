Global 6 Heaven Year · 7 Lake Month

The global Heaven year places structure, accountability and principled leadership in focus. Collective efforts tend towards order and reform, with a continuing need to balance firmness with compassion and openness.

A Lake month favours diplomacy, enjoyment and public presentation. It also asks for attention to the vulnerabilities beneath appearances, so charm and reassurance do not replace honest engagement. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through cultural expression and emotional reflection.
