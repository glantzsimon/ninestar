Global 6 Heaven Year · 8 Mountain Month

The global Heaven year places structure, accountability and principled leadership in focus. Collective efforts tend towards order and reform, with a continuing need to balance firmness with compassion and openness.

A Mountain month slows the collective pace towards consolidation and reflection. Defensive positions or delays may become more apparent; patient reassessment can reveal what needs a firmer foundation. This month introduces a contrasting pace. Its boundaries and consolidation is a shorter influence within the wider pattern, rather than evidence that the longer cycle has ended.
