Global 6 Heaven Year · 2 Soil Month

The global Heaven year places structure, accountability and principled leadership in focus. Collective efforts tend towards order and reform, with a continuing need to balance firmness with compassion and openness.

A Soil month brings practical support, maintenance and domestic or humanitarian concerns into focus. Modest cooperation and attention to existing commitments can offer steadier progress than dramatic announcements. This month introduces a contrasting pace. Its practical care and stabilisation is a shorter influence within the wider pattern, rather than evidence that the longer cycle has ended.
