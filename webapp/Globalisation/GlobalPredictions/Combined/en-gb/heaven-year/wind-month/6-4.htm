Global 6 Heaven Year · 4 Wind Month

The wider focus is on leadership, law, accountability and the way collective responsibilities are organised. Stronger boundaries or reforms can offer stability, but certainty may also narrow the space for dialogue. Ethical leadership needs both clear standards and a willingness to hear the people affected by their application. The Wind month opens the Heaven year’s firm positions to dialogue, allowing clearer rules to gain understanding through explanation rather than authority alone.

Diplomacy, education and creative exchange offer practical ways to develop understanding. Public responses may be especially sensitive to presentation, so a reassuring tone needs to be matched by clear intentions. Cooperation becomes more substantial when agreements specify what will happen next, rather than depending entirely on goodwill or a shared moment of optimism.
