The wider pattern draws attention to boundaries, inherited structures and what can still support collective life. Institutions may move cautiously, and public debate can become more guarded. Delays are an opportunity to examine foundations and consequences, provided consolidation does not become an excuse to avoid difficult questions or withdraw from necessary cooperation. The Thunder month introduces movement into the Mountain year’s slower atmosphere, offering a useful opening when initiative respects what still needs careful consideration.

Announcements, new movements or demands for reform can gather momentum quickly during the month. Enthusiasm is useful when paired with a clear purpose and a workable next step.
