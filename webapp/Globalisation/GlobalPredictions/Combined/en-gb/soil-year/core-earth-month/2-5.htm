Global 2 Soil Year · 5 Core Earth Month

The global Soil year emphasises collective rebuilding, practical care and the foundations of everyday life. Public services, domestic concerns and patient preparation form the longer background against which shorter developments unfold.

A Core Earth month brings competing pressures and unresolved questions towards a centre. A pause to clarify priorities can be more constructive than forcing decisions while public confidence is unsettled. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through collective recalibration.
