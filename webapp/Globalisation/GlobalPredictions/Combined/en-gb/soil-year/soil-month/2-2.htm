Global 2 Soil Year · 2 Soil Month

The global Soil year emphasises collective rebuilding, practical care and the foundations of everyday life. Public services, domestic concerns and patient preparation form the longer background against which shorter developments unfold.

A Soil month brings practical support, maintenance and domestic or humanitarian concerns into focus. Modest cooperation and attention to existing commitments can offer steadier progress than dramatic announcements. The repeated Soil influence reinforces these themes across both time scales; the month concentrates the longer pattern rather than introducing a different direction.
