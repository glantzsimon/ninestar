Global 2 Soil Year · 1 Water Month

The global Soil year emphasises collective rebuilding, practical care and the foundations of everyday life. Public services, domestic concerns and patient preparation form the longer background against which shorter developments unfold.

A Water month draws public attention towards what is happening beneath the surface. Communication may become quieter or less transparent, making listening and careful interpretation particularly useful. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through quiet observation and hidden undercurrents.
