Global 3 Thunder Year · 1 Water Month

The global Thunder year brings an outward surge of change, innovation and demands for reform. New voices and movements can gain momentum, while impatience and reactive public debate call for grounding and careful judgement.

A Water month draws public attention towards what is happening beneath the surface. Communication may become quieter or less transparent, making listening and careful interpretation particularly useful. This month introduces a contrasting pace. Its quiet observation and hidden undercurrents is a shorter influence within the wider pattern, rather than evidence that the longer cycle has ended.
