Global 1 Water Year · 3 Thunder Month

The global Water year sets an inward background, with uncertainty, hidden influences and questions of trust shaping collective life. Quiet reassessment and attention to emotional undercurrents matter more than the appearance of rapid progress.

A Thunder month adds a sharper burst of public expression and initiative. New ideas and demands can emerge quickly; their promise is best assessed alongside the risks of haste and overreaction. This month introduces a contrasting pace. Its activation and fresh demands for change is a shorter influence within the wider pattern, rather than evidence that the longer cycle has ended.
