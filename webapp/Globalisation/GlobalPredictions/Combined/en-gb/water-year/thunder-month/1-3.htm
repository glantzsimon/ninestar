Global 1 Water Year · 3 Thunder Month

Trust, transparency and the distribution of resources form important longer themes. Decisions made out of public view may attract concern, while cultural life can turn towards memory, uncertainty and deeper inquiry. Careful listening and patient examination of information offer a steadier response than trying to resolve an inward collective phase through louder declarations. The Thunder month brings a burst of expression into a guarded Water year; new demands need careful checking where enthusiasm outruns what is publicly known.

Announcements, new movements or demands for reform can gather momentum quickly during the month. Enthusiasm is useful when paired with a clear purpose and a workable next step. Public debate benefits from checking information and hearing quieter voices, so that an energetic opening does not become dominated by urgency, provocation or reactive decisions.
