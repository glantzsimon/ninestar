Global 4 Wind Year · 9 Fire Month

The global Wind year favours dialogue, cultural exchange and the development of emerging ideas. Collaboration can broaden possibilities, although shifting sentiment and uncertain follow-through make clarity and consistency important.

A Fire month intensifies public attention, expression and the exposure of hidden concerns. Clear insight and spectacle may arrive together, making proportion and responsible communication especially important. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through visibility and revelation.
