Global 4 Wind Year · 6 Heaven Month

Across the year, cultural exchange, education and diplomacy offer ways to develop emerging ideas. Public confidence can be sensitive to the tone of a message and the credibility of those delivering it. Collaboration benefits from clear commitments, so that openness and idealism have enough continuity to become more than a passing conversation. The Heaven month asks the Wind year’s openness to take a clearer form, balancing the need for decisions and standards with a willingness to keep listening.

The monthly emphasis falls on standards, responsibility and the exercise of authority. Clear decisions may help organise collective effort, but a firm response still needs proportion and a credible explanation. Policies and public statements benefit from scrutiny of both their principles and their effects, especially where discipline risks becoming punitive or unnecessarily inflexible.
