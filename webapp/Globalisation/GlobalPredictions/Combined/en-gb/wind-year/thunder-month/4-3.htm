Global 4 Wind Year · 3 Thunder Month

The global Wind year favours dialogue, cultural exchange and the development of emerging ideas. Collaboration can broaden possibilities, although shifting sentiment and uncertain follow-through make clarity and consistency important.

A Thunder month adds a sharper burst of public expression and initiative. New ideas and demands can emerge quickly; their promise is best assessed alongside the risks of haste and overreaction. This month broadly supports the longer rhythm, while giving it a more immediate expression through activation and fresh demands for change.
