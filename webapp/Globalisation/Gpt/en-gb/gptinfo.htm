About the 9 Star Ki AI Astrologer

The 9 Star Ki AI Astrologer is a conversational guide to the ideas and practices of 9 Star Ki. You can ask questions, explore what a chart may suggest, and learn how the system describes the changing cycles of energy.

Topics it can help you explore

Use it to understand the energies in a personal chart, look at yearly or monthly influences, explore relationship compatibility, or generate an I Ching hexagram for reflection. It can also explain the principles behind these readings and help you find your way through the 9 Star Ki knowledge base.

How to use it

Ask a specific question or describe the area you would like to understand. For a chart-based response, provide the birth details the question depends on; if the time is unknown, say so. You can ask follow-up questions to clarify a concept or explore how different influences relate to one another.

Its responses are AI-generated interpretations of 9 Star Ki. Use them as a starting point for learning and reflection, and check important chart details against the website's calculators.
