5 Core Earth Year · 9 Fire Month

A Fire month illuminates questions that may have felt unclear during a Core Earth year. Expression and insight can be welcome, but heightened visibility is best met with grounding and time to consider what you actually want to commit to. The yearly background asks for an honest look at your centre: what remains sound, what feels divided, and which patterns are ready to change. Competing demands or unresolved feelings can make direction less obvious. Give decisions time to settle, simplify where possible, and let boundaries protect the space needed for reflection rather than trying to satisfy every influence at once.

The month adds warmth and a wish to express ideas, feelings or creative work more openly. Enjoy meaningful dialogue and moments of connection, while remembering that increased visibility can also expose tender feelings. Pause before reacting to criticism, avoid overextending yourself, and leave a little quiet space to understand what this brighter period is revealing.
