5 Core Earth Year · 6 Heaven Month

A Heaven month offers structure and clearer judgment within a Core Earth year of recalibration. Use it to organise responsibilities and define boundaries, while leaving room to revise conclusions as your deeper priorities become clearer. The yearly background asks for an honest look at your centre: what remains sound, what feels divided, and which patterns are ready to change. Competing demands or unresolved feelings can make direction less obvious. Give decisions time to settle, simplify where possible, and let boundaries protect the space needed for reflection rather than trying to satisfy every influence at once.

The month brings an opportunity to refine your schedule, clarify commitments and communicate more directly. Use that precision with warmth, especially if certainty begins to harden into judgement or perfectionism. Making room for music, beauty or an honest conversation can help you remain emotionally available while still giving your work and responsibilities a clear structure.
