A Thunder month introduces movement and fresh ideas into a Core Earth year of reassessment. The yearly background asks for an honest look at your centre: what remains sound, what feels divided, and which patterns are ready to change. Competing demands or unresolved feelings can make direction less obvious. Give decisions time to settle, simplify where possible, and let boundaries protect the space needed for reflection rather than trying to satisfy every influence at once.

The month can make inspiration feel immediate, with a stronger urge to speak, move and experiment. Give that enthusiasm a constructive outlet through a manageable project, creative work or purposeful activity.
