9 Fire Year · 7 Lake Month

A Lake month softens a Fire year’s intensity with pleasure, humour and more relaxed connection. Enjoy being with others without turning every encounter into a performance, and let appreciation restore a more natural relationship with expression. The year brings greater visibility, creativity and emotional expression, with opportunities to share what has been developing more privately. Insight can arrive quickly, and hidden concerns may become easier to recognise. Give brightness a steady foundation through rest and sincerity, so that being seen remains a meaningful expression of yourself rather than a pressure to perform.

The month encourages easeful company, humour and an appreciation of ordinary pleasures. Beneath a charming or carefree exterior, however, feelings may be more delicate than they appear. Make choices that leave you nourished rather than merely entertained, and allow private reflection alongside sociability so that genuine needs are not lost in a wish to keep everything pleasant.
