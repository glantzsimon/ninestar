A Soil month brings grounding and practical care to a Fire year’s brightness. Supporting routines, close relationships and quieter responsibilities can sustain expression, especially when the wish to be seen begins to outpace your capacity. The year brings greater visibility, creativity and emotional expression, with opportunities to share what has been developing more privately. Insight can arrive quickly, and hidden concerns may become easier to recognise. Give brightness a steady foundation through rest and sincerity, so that being seen remains a meaningful expression of yourself rather than a pressure to perform.

At the monthly level, hesitant hope and a need for reassurance may sit side by side. Preparing your environment, reviewing plans and sharing a quiet moment with someone familiar can help intentions take shape. Resist comparing this slower rhythm with more outward periods; caring for the basics now gives future activity somewhere dependable to take root.
