6 Heaven Year · 6 Heaven Month

Heaven across year and month emphasises order, responsibility and decisive focus. This can support careful completion and clear boundaries, while kindness, flexibility and willingness to listen prevent conviction from becoming rigidity. The year favours responsibility, organisation and bringing your choices into line with your values. There can be satisfaction in refining work, keeping commitments and making clear decisions. Yet strength is most useful when it remains flexible: listen to other perspectives, soften blunt communication and leave room for support rather than carrying every responsibility alone.

The month brings an opportunity to refine your schedule, clarify commitments and communicate more directly. Use that precision with warmth, especially if certainty begins to harden into judgement or perfectionism. Making room for music, beauty or an honest conversation can help you remain emotionally available while still giving your work and responsibilities a clear structure.
