6 Heaven Year · 7 Lake Month

A Lake month brings ease and human warmth to a Heaven year’s purposeful effort. Make room to appreciate progress and enjoy connection, allowing pleasure and sincere conversation to soften an overly demanding relationship with responsibility. The year favours responsibility, organisation and bringing your choices into line with your values. There can be satisfaction in refining work, keeping commitments and making clear decisions. Yet strength is most useful when it remains flexible: listen to other perspectives, soften blunt communication and leave room for support rather than carrying every responsibility alone.

The month encourages easeful company, humour and an appreciation of ordinary pleasures. Beneath a charming or carefree exterior, however, feelings may be more delicate than they appear. Make choices that leave you nourished rather than merely entertained, and allow private reflection alongside sociability so that genuine needs are not lost in a wish to keep everything pleasant.
