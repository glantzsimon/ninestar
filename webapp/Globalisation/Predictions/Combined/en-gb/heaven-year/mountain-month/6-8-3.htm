A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.

Today’s Thunder influence brings movement into the month’s contained stillness, offering a brief chance to loosen inertia through a manageable action. Channel a fresh impulse into a clear priority, taking time to consider its consequences before acting. Direct the fresh energy into one constructive action, whether that is creative work, movement or a task you have postponed. Excitement can be useful without becoming a reason to rush a decision.
