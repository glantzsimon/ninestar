6 Heaven Year · 8 Mountain Month

A Mountain month introduces a contained, reflective interval into a Heaven year’s strong direction. Step back to review your commitments and approach, keeping thoughtfulness distinct from stubbornness and leaving room for trusted feedback. The year favours responsibility, organisation and bringing your choices into line with your values. There can be satisfaction in refining work, keeping commitments and making clear decisions. Yet strength is most useful when it remains flexible: listen to other perspectives, soften blunt communication and leave room for support rather than carrying every responsibility alone.

The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.
