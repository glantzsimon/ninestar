This month lends itself to thoughtful conversation, creative collaboration and developing relationships with tact. Emotional openness can be refreshing, but changing moods or too many invitations may scatter your attention. Keep returning to the commitments that matter, balance sociability with quiet time, and allow flexibility to support your direction rather than continually change it.

Today’s Core Earth influence asks you to centre yourself amid the month’s many connections, clarifying which demands belong with you. Identify what is genuinely yours to carry and give yourself permission to set down an unnecessary obligation. Simplify the immediate demands on your attention and notice where you are being drawn into too many roles. A boundary, a pause before deciding or a quiet return to essentials may bring more clarity than another commitment. If an old feeling surfaces, give it room to be understood before trying to act on it.
