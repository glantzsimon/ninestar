7 Lake Year · 7 Lake Month

Lake across year and month emphasises appreciation, beauty and heartfelt enjoyment. Let ease deepen into gratitude and genuine connection, while maintaining enough honesty and moderation to avoid slipping into distraction or overindulgence. The year has the quality of a harvest, inviting gratitude for earlier efforts and more space for friendship, romance, beauty and enjoyment. Rewards are best appreciated without turning ease into excess or allowing appearances to replace sincerity. Alongside pleasure, make room to reflect on what the cycle has taught you and what you are gradually ready to release.

The month encourages easeful company, humour and an appreciation of ordinary pleasures. Beneath a charming or carefree exterior, however, feelings may be more delicate than they appear. Make choices that leave you nourished rather than merely entertained, and allow private reflection alongside sociability so that genuine needs are not lost in a wish to keep everything pleasant.
