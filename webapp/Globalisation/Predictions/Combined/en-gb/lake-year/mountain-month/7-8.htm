7 Lake Year · 8 Mountain Month

A Mountain month draws the sociability of a Lake year into a quieter, more reflective space. Let enjoyment become simpler and more inward for a while, keeping gentle contact with loved ones as you consider what is ready to be released. The year has the quality of a harvest, inviting gratitude for earlier efforts and more space for friendship, romance, beauty and enjoyment. Rewards are best appreciated without turning ease into excess or allowing appearances to replace sincerity. Alongside pleasure, make room to reflect on what the cycle has taught you and what you are gradually ready to release.

The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.
