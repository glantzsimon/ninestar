7 Lake Year · 2 Soil Month

A Soil month brings practical care to a Lake year’s atmosphere of ease. Tending your home, routines and close relationships can make enjoyment more sustainable, while helping you appreciate what supports the life you are savouring. The year has the quality of a harvest, inviting gratitude for earlier efforts and more space for friendship, romance, beauty and enjoyment. Rewards are best appreciated without turning ease into excess or allowing appearances to replace sincerity. Alongside pleasure, make room to reflect on what the cycle has taught you and what you are gradually ready to release.

At the monthly level, hesitant hope and a need for reassurance may sit side by side. Preparing your environment, reviewing plans and sharing a quiet moment with someone familiar can help intentions take shape. Resist comparing this slower rhythm with more outward periods; caring for the basics now gives future activity somewhere dependable to take root.
