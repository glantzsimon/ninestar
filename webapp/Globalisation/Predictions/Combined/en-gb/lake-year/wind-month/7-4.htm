7 Lake Year · 4 Wind Month

A Wind month brings creative exchange and relational growth to a Lake year. Enjoy the flow of conversation and collaboration, keeping sincerity and continuity at the heart of connections that might otherwise remain only pleasant encounters. The year has the quality of a harvest, inviting gratitude for earlier efforts and more space for friendship, romance, beauty and enjoyment. Rewards are best appreciated without turning ease into excess or allowing appearances to replace sincerity. Alongside pleasure, make room to reflect on what the cycle has taught you and what you are gradually ready to release.

This month lends itself to thoughtful conversation, creative collaboration and developing relationships with tact. Emotional openness can be refreshing, but changing moods or too many invitations may scatter your attention. Keep returning to the commitments that matter, balance sociability with quiet time, and allow flexibility to support your direction rather than continually change it.
