8 Mountain Year · 6 Heaven Month

A Heaven month brings clarity and structure to a Mountain year’s reflective pause. Reviewing commitments can be productive, provided that firmer conclusions do not close off the listening and flexibility that deeper reflection requires. The yearly background encourages consolidation and honest reflection, as though life were asking you to spend some time in a quiet mountain shelter. Motivation may fluctuate, and pressure can make resistance stronger. Move steadily, keep communication clear and maintain gentle contact with trusted people, allowing any important change to grow from your values rather than temporary restlessness.

The month brings an opportunity to refine your schedule, clarify commitments and communicate more directly. Use that precision with warmth, especially if certainty begins to harden into judgement or perfectionism. Making room for music, beauty or an honest conversation can help you remain emotionally available while still giving your work and responsibilities a clear structure.
