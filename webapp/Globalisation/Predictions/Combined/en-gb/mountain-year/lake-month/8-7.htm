8 Mountain Year · 7 Lake Month

A Lake month offers warmth and lightness within a Mountain year’s more withdrawn atmosphere. Gentle pleasure and trusted company can help you emerge from your cave in small ways, without asking you to abandon the quiet you still need. The yearly background encourages consolidation and honest reflection, as though life were asking you to spend some time in a quiet mountain shelter. Motivation may fluctuate, and pressure can make resistance stronger. Move steadily, keep communication clear and maintain gentle contact with trusted people, allowing any important change to grow from your values rather than temporary restlessness.

The month encourages easeful company, humour and an appreciation of ordinary pleasures. Beneath a charming or carefree exterior, however, feelings may be more delicate than they appear. Make choices that leave you nourished rather than merely entertained, and allow private reflection alongside sociability so that genuine needs are not lost in a wish to keep everything pleasant.
