8 Mountain Year · 8 Mountain Month

Mountain across year and month concentrates stillness, reflection and the desire for solitude. Honour the pause, while making small efforts to move, communicate and remain receptive so that rest does not harden into isolation or resistance. The yearly background encourages consolidation and honest reflection, as though life were asking you to spend some time in a quiet mountain shelter. Motivation may fluctuate, and pressure can make resistance stronger. Move steadily, keep communication clear and maintain gentle contact with trusted people, allowing any important change to grow from your values rather than temporary restlessness.

The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.
