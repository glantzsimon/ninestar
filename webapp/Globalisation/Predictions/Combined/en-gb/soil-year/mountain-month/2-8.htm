2 Soil Year · 8 Mountain Month

A Mountain month makes a Soil year feel more contained, drawing attention to what needs settling before progress can resume. Reflect on your foundations, but keep small routines and gentle contact with others alive so that preparation does not become indefinite withdrawal. The year is concerned with preparation and foundations, much like a seed beginning to stir before spring. Clearing clutter, gathering resources and refining plans can make later growth more sustainable. If progress feels modest or confidence wavers, allow trusted relationships and simple routines to support you; quiet groundwork still has value when there is little outward recognition.

The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.
