2 Soil Year · 3 Thunder Month

A Thunder month stirs the patient groundwork of a Soil year into more immediate movement. There may be enough enthusiasm to try a first step, but keep it manageable and connected to the foundations you are still building. The year is concerned with preparation and foundations, much like a seed beginning to stir before spring. Clearing clutter, gathering resources and refining plans can make later growth more sustainable. If progress feels modest or confidence wavers, allow trusted relationships and simple routines to support you; quiet groundwork still has value when there is little outward recognition.

The month can make inspiration feel immediate, with a stronger urge to speak, move and experiment. Give that enthusiasm a constructive outlet through a manageable project, creative work or purposeful activity. Check details before committing and listen before reacting; you can welcome fresh momentum without expecting yourself or everyone around you to move at the same speed.
