A Mountain month creates a pause within a Thunder year’s appetite for movement. The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. Today’s Core Earth influence may bring unresolved concerns into the month’s stillness; find your centre before deciding that something must change immediately. A boundary, a pause before deciding or a quiet return to essentials may bring more clarity than another commitment.
