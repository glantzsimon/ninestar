A Mountain month creates a pause within a Thunder year’s appetite for movement. The contrast may feel frustrating, but a deliberate review can help you recognise where action is useful and where impatience is doing the driving. The yearly background carries the urgency of early spring, bringing fresh ideas, confidence and a wish to set plans in motion. Learning, travel or creative ventures may appeal, but enthusiasm needs enough structure to become lasting progress. Leave room to listen, check details and rest, especially when impatience makes everything seem equally urgent.

The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.
