3 Thunder Year · 3 Thunder Month

Thunder across year and month concentrates the impulse to act, create and begin. The opportunity is to turn inspiration into tangible movement; choose a clear direction and allow enough patience for details, other people and unfinished work. The yearly background carries the urgency of early spring, bringing fresh ideas, confidence and a wish to set plans in motion. Learning, travel or creative ventures may appeal, but enthusiasm needs enough structure to become lasting progress. Leave room to listen, check details and rest, especially when impatience makes everything seem equally urgent.

The month can make inspiration feel immediate, with a stronger urge to speak, move and experiment. Give that enthusiasm a constructive outlet through a manageable project, creative work or purposeful activity. Check details before committing and listen before reacting; you can welcome fresh momentum without expecting yourself or everyone around you to move at the same speed.
