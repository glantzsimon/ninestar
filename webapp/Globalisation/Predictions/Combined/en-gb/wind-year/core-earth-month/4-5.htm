4 Wind Year · 5 Core Earth Month

A Core Earth month brings a period of reassessment within a year of growth and connection. If possibilities pull you in different directions, return to your values and give yourself space to decide which relationships and projects deserve continued attention. The year supports continuing growth through communication, relationships and the careful nurturing of what is already underway. Your voice may carry further, opening opportunities for writing, teaching or collaboration. The challenge is consistency: a change of mood or an attractive new possibility need not mean abandoning the path you have already begun to cultivate.

During the month, recurring feelings or interactions may reveal where your life needs greater balance. You do not have to settle every question immediately or be available to everyone who asks for help. Observe what returns, give yourself time to process it, and favour simple commitments and clear boundaries over a quick solution chosen merely to escape uncertainty.
