4 Wind Year · 9 Fire Month

A Fire month brings the growth of a Wind year into brighter view. Sharing your work or expressing what matters can feel more natural, provided you stay anchored in the relationships and steady effort that support your wider direction. The year supports continuing growth through communication, relationships and the careful nurturing of what is already underway. Your voice may carry further, opening opportunities for writing, teaching or collaboration. The challenge is consistency: a change of mood or an attractive new possibility need not mean abandoning the path you have already begun to cultivate.

The month adds warmth and a wish to express ideas, feelings or creative work more openly. Enjoy meaningful dialogue and moments of connection, while remembering that increased visibility can also expose tender feelings. Pause before reacting to criticism, avoid overextending yourself, and leave a little quiet space to understand what this brighter period is revealing.
