4 Wind Year · 8 Mountain Month · 5 Core Earth Day

A Mountain month asks for stillness within a Wind year’s more fluid, communicative rhythm. Allow space to review what you have been growing, while maintaining a few gentle connections so that a useful pause does not become a complete retreat. The year supports continuing growth through communication, relationships and the careful nurturing of what is already underway. Your voice may carry further, opening opportunities for writing, teaching or collaboration. The challenge is consistency: a change of mood or an attractive new possibility need not mean abandoning the path you have already begun to cultivate. The month may bring uncertainty, less desire to communicate and an urge to retreat into familiar surroundings. Make that pause restorative by reducing pressure and considering changes carefully. A simple message, quiet visit or request for support can preserve closeness without demanding a busy social life; solitude is more helpful when it leaves a door open to people you trust.

Today’s Core Earth influence may bring unresolved concerns into the month’s stillness; find your centre before deciding that something must change immediately. Return to your central commitments and make space to notice which connections are strengthening or scattering your focus. Simplify the immediate demands on your attention and notice where you are being drawn into too many roles. A boundary, a pause before deciding or a quiet return to essentials may bring more clarity than another commitment. If an old feeling surfaces, give it room to be understood before trying to act on it.
