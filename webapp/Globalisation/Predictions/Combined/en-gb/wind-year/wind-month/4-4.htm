4 Wind Year · 4 Wind Month

Wind across the year and month favours communication, collaboration and organic development. The challenge is to remain rooted while opportunities multiply; nurture a few meaningful connections and let flexibility serve continuity. The year supports continuing growth through communication, relationships and the careful nurturing of what is already underway. Your voice may carry further, opening opportunities for writing, teaching or collaboration. The challenge is consistency: a change of mood or an attractive new possibility need not mean abandoning the path you have already begun to cultivate.

This month lends itself to thoughtful conversation, creative collaboration and developing relationships with tact. Emotional openness can be refreshing, but changing moods or too many invitations may scatter your attention. Keep returning to the commitments that matter, balance sociability with quiet time, and allow flexibility to support your direction rather than continually change it.
